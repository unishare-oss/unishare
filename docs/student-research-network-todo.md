# Student Research Network TODO

## Concept

Build a student research participation network inside UniShare so university students can find real participants for surveys, interviews, and class research assignments.

This should solve a practical assignment problem for courses like:

- GEN
- LNG
- marketing
- communication
- research-heavy classes

Core positioning:

> Help students find real participants for surveys and interviews faster.

This should not be positioned as a shortcut for cheating or fake response trading.

## Product Direction

The feature should focus on structured participant matching rather than generic social posting.

Primary use cases:

- students need survey respondents before a deadline
- students need interview volunteers for class assignments
- students want to help others and receive help back
- students need filters to find the right participant type

## MVP TODO

### 1. Survey Help Board

Allow students to post:

- survey title
- class / assignment type
- target participant profile
- deadline
- estimated completion time
- survey link
- number of responses still needed
- anonymity disclosure

### 2. Interview Volunteer Board

Allow students to post:

- interview topic
- course or assignment type
- preferred participant profile
- online or in-person format
- expected duration
- available time slots
- preferred location for offline meetings

### 3. Smart Matching

Support filtering and discovery by:

- university
- faculty
- year
- language
- gender if relevant to research scope
- international or local student
- availability

### 4. Help Exchange

Add lightweight mutual-help actions:

- "I answered yours"
- "I can help with interview"
- help-for-help matching
- simple contribution tracking

Goal:

- encourage reciprocal participation without turning the platform into a paid response market

### 5. Trust Layer

Add basic trust and moderation systems:

- university email verification
- reporter / block flow
- visible profile reputation
- completed-help count
- basic moderation for abuse and spam

Goal:

- make survey and interview requests feel safer and more credible

### 6. In-Person Safety Rules

If interview matching supports offline meetings:

- only allow public meeting spots
- avoid exposing private personal information publicly
- support anonymous first contact through the platform if possible
- require clear consent before recording audio or video
- add a reporting path for unsafe behavior

Goal:

- reduce personal safety risk for students meeting through the platform

## Legal And Safety Rules

Allowed:

- finding real survey respondents
- finding interview volunteers
- academic research participant matching
- language practice interviews
- project-related student feedback

Not allowed:

- fake responses
- fabricated interview data
- selling completed assignments
- impersonation
- coercive reward scams
- collecting sensitive personal data without clear disclosure

Every post should clearly show:

- purpose of the survey or interview
- estimated time required
- whether participation is anonymous
- whether it is for class, research, or a student project
- deadline

## Product Guardrails

Important principle:

This feature must help students find real participants, not artificially inflate research quality with dishonest or low-integrity responses.

Avoid product language such as:

- boost your responses fast
- finish assignments easily
- trade answers

Prefer product language such as:

- find real participants
- reach relevant students faster
- get help for surveys and interviews
- help others and get help back

## Rollout Notes

Suggested rollout order:

1. survey help board
2. interview volunteer board
3. smart matching filters
4. help exchange actions
5. trust and moderation tools
6. safe in-person coordination
