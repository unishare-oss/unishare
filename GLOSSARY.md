# Unishare

A study-sharing platform where university students share notes, prepare for exams and help
classmates inside their own university.

## Pages and surfaces

**Today**:
The signed-in student's home page: their upcoming exams at the top and their feed below.
_Avoid_: Home, Dashboard, Feed page

**Feed**:
The filterable list of posts from the student's university, shown on Today below the exams.
_Avoid_: Timeline, stream

**Calm page**:
A page made for reading (a note, a course, sharing a note): flat, quiet, serif note text.
_Avoid_: Reading mode, minimal page

**Playful surface**:
A place made for practice and return visits (Today, quizzes): bold buttons, motion and Noto.
_Avoid_: Gamified page, fun mode

**Noto**:
Unishare's mascot, an animated orange note. It appears on playful surfaces and on the one playful card a calm page may have (the course page's Up next card); the app logo uses a still drawing of it.
_Avoid_: Mascot, buddy, character

## Content

**Module**:
One of a course's numbered parts; each post belongs to one module.
_Avoid_: Week, unit, chapter
